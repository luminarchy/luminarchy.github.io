About

As both a writer and a computer scientist, I believe that creativity is the most vital driving force behind innovation and technology, being inherent to human nature and embedded within our programming. After all, the art of mathematics originated from philosophy.

I graduated from Rice University with a Bachelor's of Arts in Computer Science and English with a minor in Asian Studies. As my degree states, my wide range of interests go from deep learning and adversarial networks to the forms and metrics of poetry to non-duality in Chinese philosophy, literature, and religion.

Currently I am pursuing graduate studies at USC Viterbi, majoring in Computer Science with a specialization in Vision and AI. During this time, I hope to learn more about how AI and technology can enable wellbeing and creativity in society whether it be through fields such as medical imaging or autonomous systems. As a computer scientist, I hope to bring studies in the arts and humanities to the tech field, providing creative solutions to complex problems.

My current interests are in the fields of computer vision, deep learning, and agentic AI, but as an avid learner, I am always exploring and adapting.

AMD 2025 AI Voice Agent

An AI Agent application using MCP (model context protocol) and Open WebUI using gpu acceleration on AMD’s MI300x. Uses a server to connect a xLAM model to any labeled dataset for informed, specific task execution, and sends receives requests using voice and audio. These requests are processed using the xLAM model and can request sampling from the llm if necessary. This project was demoed at the 2025 AMD Partner Summit using a database of PoetryFoundation poems as an example.

An AI Vision agent that uses a Phi multimodal model and a document layout segmentation model to segment text, headers, and images from a document for text classification.
